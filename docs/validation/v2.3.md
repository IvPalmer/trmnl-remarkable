## Device validation

**The reMarkable 1 and reMarkable 2 paths have not been exercised on
hardware.** This project is developed on a Paper Pro, and no rM1 or rM2 was
available for this release. The device baseline remains the v2.1 record:
reMarkable Paper Pro (`reMarkable Ferrari`), aarch64, reMarkable OS 3.27.3.0,
kernel `6.12.49+git-imx8mm-ferrari-g68b95e858a0a`.

What that means for an owner of one of those tablets: the installer will now
proceed rather than refuse, and the checks it performs before writing anything
are the same ones the Paper Pro path uses. Everything after that point — the
arm32 XOVI injection, AppLoad launching the app, the refresh loop, RTC wake, and
uninstall or recovery — is untested on that hardware. Treat 2.3.0 on a
reMarkable 1 or 2 as a first run, keep the tablet backed up, and report what
happens.

## Known limitations

Carried forward:

- reMarkable OS 3.26.x remains installer-allowed but has not been exercised on
  hardware. Only 3.27.3.0 has.
- A multi-day battery runtime figure cannot be produced during a release; the
  in-app battery test is the supported way for an owner to measure their own.
- The colour palette used for dithering is an approximation of the panel's
  displayable set. It is overridable via `dither_palette` in `config.json` for
  anyone who measures a better one.
- The tablet cannot update itself. The update check only reports that a newer
  release exists; installing it still needs the Windows installer.
- Disabling the weekly brightness schedule does not restore system-brightness
  mode automatically.

New in this release:

- No reMarkable 1 or reMarkable 2 hardware validation, as described above.
- The reMarkable 1 firmware window (3.20.x through 3.27.x) is set from the last
  release line that device shipped rather than from a firmware confirmed on
  hardware. If AppLoad turns out to need a narrower range there, the window
  should be tightened.
- The 16-level grey ramp used as the dither target on the reMarkable 1 and 2 is
  the panel's nominal set, not a measured one. It is overridable the same way
  the colour palette is.
